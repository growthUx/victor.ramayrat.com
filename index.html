07 / Frequently asked

What hiring managers ask me most.

What roles are you targeting?
Senior Manager and above in Web Operations, Digital Experience, CMS/Martech Architecture, or adjacent digital platform leadership. The common thread: roles where the web property is treated as a product with a revenue mandate, and where the leader is expected to both set strategy and understand the build.
What's the through-line of your career?
Taking ambiguous strategic problems and turning them into shipped systems. Every chapter, from PwC's global GTM web platforms to Tradeshift's web operations to Redkix's growth engine to my own two platforms, is the same motion at a different scale: define the strategy, architect the system, ship it, and measure it against revenue.
Why did you spend the last stretch as a founder?
After a decade operating for others, I built and now run two production platforms end to end: Orbytt and Socient. It made me a sharper leader. I've owned the P&L, the architecture, the roadmap, and the operations with no one to escalate to. I'm bringing that full-ownership operating mode back inside an organization, and my ventures run without requiring my full-time attention.
How do you use AI in your work?
As infrastructure, not garnish. I build agentic workflows for content operations, QA, personalization, and reporting, all in production for real clients and my own platforms. For a team, that translates into a leader who can redesign the operating model around AI rather than bolt tools onto old processes.
Are you open to hands-on work, or leadership only?
Both, deliberately. I lead through systems and people, but I've never stopped building. Teams get a manager who can review the architecture, unblock the implementation, and represent the work credibly to executives.
