07 / Frequently asked

What a marketing leader will ask me.

What would you do in the first 90 days?
Weeks 1 to 2: I run my 50-check audit on eve.legal and on two or three competitors. Weeks 2 to 4: I fix the quick wins, such as schema, crawler access, llms.txt, and profiles on review sites. Month 2: I set a baseline for citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Month 3: I ship the first set of content built on the questions plaintiff attorneys ask, and I report the change.
How do you measure AI visibility?
I use a fixed set of real buyer prompts. I run them on a set schedule across the five AI surfaces. I track mentions, citations, share of voice against competitors, and the URLs that the AI tools cite. Then I connect this data to Search Console, GA4, and sign-ups in the CRM. The method works in tools such as Profound or Peec AI, or in a custom build.
Can you write for attorneys?
Attorneys read fast and check every claim. I write short, plain sentences, I give a source for each fact, and I remove hype. I learn the domain from the people who know it: Eve's customers, sales team, and legal staff. I write for review, not around it.
Do you have legal tech experience?
Not inside a legal tech company. I have 10+ years in B2B SaaS and enterprise products, including PwC's Risk products for compliance buyers. The AI visibility method is the same in each market. The prompts, the sources, and the communities change, and I learn them fast.
Have you managed contractors and agencies?
Yes. I manage writers, developers, and agencies for my own properties and for client teams. I give each one a clear brief, a quality bar, and a deadline, and I review the work before it ships.
Can you code?
Yes. I write JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Next.js, and I ship schema and site changes myself. The team does not need to wait for engineering to test an idea.
