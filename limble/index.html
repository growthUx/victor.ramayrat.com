07 / Frequently asked

What hiring managers ask me most.

Have you run Webflow before?
Yes. I build and manage websites across WordPress, Webflow, and headless CMS platforms, including the design system, content collections, and publishing workflow. This experience lets me move fast in Webflow without a learning curve.
How do you approach technical SEO?
I treat site architecture, structured data, and Core Web Vitals as a working budget, not a one-time audit. I review them on a set schedule, tied to real traffic and conversion data from GA4 and Search Console.
What is your experience with AI search (AEO/GEO)?
Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) are core to my studio, Socient. I write and structure content so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini can find it, understand it, and cite it.
What's the through-line of your career?
I take ambiguous strategic problems and turn them into shipped systems. Every chapter, from PwC's global GTM web platforms to Tradeshift's web operations to Redkix's growth engine to my own two platforms, is the same motion at a different scale: set the strategy, build the system, ship it, and measure it against revenue.
Are you open to hands-on execution, not just strategy?
Yes, by design. I set strategy and I still build. In this role, that means I can design the site roadmap and also work inside Webflow, write the SEO brief, and ship the fix myself when speed matters.
How do you use AI in your work?
As infrastructure, not a add-on. I build agentic workflows for content operations, QA, and reporting, all in production for real clients and my own platforms. For Limble, that means a manager who can redesign the workflow around AI instead of bolting tools onto an old process.
